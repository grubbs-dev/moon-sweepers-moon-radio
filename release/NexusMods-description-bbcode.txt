[size=5][b]Moon Radio[/b][/size]

Bring shared internet radio to Moon Sweepers stations and vehicles.

Moon Radio adds a native-styled [b]RADIO R[/b] shortcut to the game's HUD. Press [b]R[/b] to open an in-game radio panel, choose a station by genre, search for a station, or paste a direct HTTPS MP3 stream. Radio audio is positional, allowing nearby players to hear the same station from its station or vehicle emitter.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]In-game radio panel matching the Moon Sweepers interface style.
[*][b]R[/b] radio shortcut inside stations.
[*][b]R[/b] radio shortcut while seated in eligible vehicles.
[*]Internet radio station and genre discovery.
[*]Direct HTTPS MP3 stream support.
[*]Independent radio state for every station and eligible vehicle.
[*]Station audio range of 200 metres.
[*]Vehicle audio audible from outside up to 15 metres away.
[*]Multiplayer synchronization for station identity and playback state.
[*]Separate stations and vehicles can play different radios.
[*]No external music player window required.
[*]No video playback required.
[*]Push mowers are excluded from vehicle radio controls.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]

1. Close Moon Sweepers.
2. Install BepInEx and the MoonSweepers BepInEx Patcher.
3. Extract the archive into the folder containing MoonSweepers.exe.
4. Confirm the DLL is located at:

[code]BepInEx/plugins/MoonRadio/MoonRadio.dll[/code]

5. Launch Moon Sweepers.
6. Enter a station or eligible vehicle and press R.

Do not install multiple copies of MoonRadio.dll. For updates, remove the old BepInEx/plugins/MoonRadio folder first.

[size=4][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Moon Radio synchronizes the radio emitter rather than transmitting music data between players. Each listener fetches the selected stream locally.

All players who want to hear synchronized radio need Moon Radio installed. Players may hear different audio if a station is unavailable in their region or blocked by their network.

Station radios are audible within 200 metres. Vehicle radios are audible from outside the vehicle within 15 metres.

[size=4][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]

If the RADIO R shortcut is missing, verify the DLL path, remove duplicate copies, and restart the game.

If pressing R does nothing, close other menus and make sure the game window is focused.

If a station does not play, try another station or genre. Direct URLs must point to an HTTPS MP3 stream, not a normal YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, or webpage URL.

For multiplayer, all players need the mod installed and must be within the radio emitter's range.

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Normal YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Music links are not direct audio stream URLs.
[*]Internet radio stations may go offline or change formats.
[*]Each multiplayer listener downloads the stream independently.
[/list]
